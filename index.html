Your privacy at Ayyanar Jewellery

Website privacy notice · Updated 15 November 2026

This notice explains what the Ayyanar Jewellery website demo processes when you browse designs, use the stylist, upload a photo, or start a voice call.

Photos and camera try-on

Hand, face, or neckline photos chosen for try-on are processed in your browser for face/hand landmarks and an approximate placement preview. An old-gold photo is only shown as a local preview; the estimate uses the weight and purity you enter and does not determine them from the photo. The website does not upload these photos to Ayyanar Jewellery or Vapi. Previews exist temporarily on your device; closing the chat removes the visible try-on preview, and refreshing or closing the page clears browser memory. Camera access begins only after you allow it, and you can stop the camera at any time. This feature does not identify who you are.

Voice calls and chat messages

If you choose to start a Vapi voice call, your microphone audio is sent to Vapi and its configured service providers so the assistant can respond. Voice transcripts are shown in the chat window. Typed messages sent while the call is active are also sent to Vapi. Vapi may process or retain call data according to the assistant’s recording, transcript, and retention settings. The website does not send your try-on photo or camera stream to Vapi.

When no Vapi call is active, the text stylist uses the website’s built-in sample replies and catalogue suggestions in your browser. Those messages are not sent to Vapi. The catalogue and price estimate features are demonstrations and do not place an order or submit your contact details.

Hosting and external services

The site is hosted by Netlify. The browser also loads some jewellery images and the optional hand/face landmark software from third-party services. Those providers may receive standard technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details, when your browser fetches their content. Their own privacy notices apply to their services. Opening an external rate or image link takes you to that provider’s website.

Your choices

  • You can browse without uploading a photo or enabling your camera.
  • You can decline microphone access and continue using the site without voice calls.
  • You can close the chat to remove its temporary try-on previews.

Questions

For privacy questions or requests, contact Ayyanar Jewellery through the shop’s official contact channels. This demo site does not currently collect account, payment-card, or order details.
